Chinese Sweet and Sour Pork

Ingredients:
- 12 ounces (340 g) pork tenderloin, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 2 tablespoons (30 ml) Shaoxing wine
- 1 large egg plus 1 egg white
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/8 teaspoon ground white pepper
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) neutral oil (for the marinade)
- 2 teaspoons potato starch or cornstarch (for the marinade)
- 1/2 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt (for the marinade)
- 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons potato starch or cornstarch (for the dredge)
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder (for the dredge)
- 1/2 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt (for the dredge)
- 3 tablespoons of the marinade, from above (for the dredge)
- 4 cups neutral oil, for frying
- 170 g pineapple, peeled, cored, and cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 large green bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 large red bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 5 tablespoons ketchup (for the sauce)
- 2 tablespoons distilled white vinegar (for the sauce)
- 3 tablespoons water, divided (for the sauce)
- 2 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon granulated sugar (for the sauce)
- 1/4 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt (for the sauce)
- 1/2 teaspoon cornstarch (for the sauce)
- White rice, for serving (optional)

Instructions:
1. Whisk Shaoxing wine, egg, egg white, baking soda, white pepper, oil, potato starch, and salt to make the marinade. Add pork and toss to coat. Let sit at least 15 minutes, up to 2 hours refrigerated.
2. Whisk potato starch, baking powder, and salt for the dredge. Add 3 tablespoons of the marinade and stir with a fork until small, pea-size clumps form.
3. Lift each piece of pork from the marinade, letting excess liquid drip off, then press into the dredge to coat as much of the pork as possible. Place the coated pork on a baking sheet.
4. Heat oil to 325°F (165°C) in a large wok. Fry the pork in batches until the coating sets and just begins to take on color, about 2 minutes per batch. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
5. Increase the oil temperature to 375°F (190°C). Fry the pork again in batches until deeply golden and crisp, about 1 to 1.5 minutes per batch. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate.
6. Add the bell peppers and pineapple to the hot oil and fry until just slightly softened and blistered, about 1 minute. Drain on a separate paper towel-lined plate.
7. Pour off the frying oil, reserving 1 tablespoon in the wok. Reduce heat to medium. Whisk ketchup, vinegar, 2 tablespoons water, sugar, and salt, then add to the wok and cook, stirring, until it begins to bubble.
8. Whisk cornstarch with the remaining 1 tablespoon water to make a slurry. Stir the slurry into the bubbling sauce and cook, stirring, until thickened and glossy, about 30 seconds.
9. Add the fried pork, bell peppers, and pineapple to the wok. Toss quickly but thoroughly to coat evenly in the sauce. Serve immediately.
